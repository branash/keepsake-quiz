Doting privacy policy

Doting exists to hold the private details of someone you love. We built it so that we never see them.

What stays on your phone

Everything you type, tap, or photograph in Doting is stored on your device only. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, and no analytics. We cannot read your cards, and neither can anyone else with access to our systems, because our systems do not have them.

The quiz

When you send a quiz, the questions travel inside the link itself. When your partner answers, their browser encrypts the answers with a key that exists only in that link, and the encrypted result is held briefly on our server until your app collects it, which also deletes it. We hold ciphertext we cannot decrypt, usually for minutes. Unclaimed replies are deleted after 60 days.

What we could see

Our quiz server (hosted on Supabase) necessarily sees standard connection metadata such as IP addresses when a quiz is submitted, and GitHub Pages sees the same when the quiz page loads. Neither is logged by us or connected to any identity.

Location and weather

If you turn suggestions on, your location is processed on your phone to notice arrivals at places you saved. Coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo only to fetch the weather, with nothing identifying attached. Location history is never stored or transmitted.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We receive no payment details.

Deleting your data

Settings → Your data → Delete everything removes every card, photo, and list from your device permanently. Because we keep nothing, there is nothing to ask us to delete.
